# Bitwork v0.90 research continuation

Mission: investigate general intelligence through compact causal learning, exact reasoning, unknown-world uncertainty, externally gated claims, predictive abstraction and genuine cross-domain transfer. **Only BGR may be modified; Perci, Pegasus, Cortex and other systems are out of scope.** User explicitly defers language models and developing our own natural-language subsystem until the reasoning research is ready.

Current v0.90:
- New symbolic finite-prefix support up to n=4 hidden deterministic Moore states using first-visit state naming; no full n=4 catalogue of ~16.8m labelled machines. Missing sensor bits remain set-valued. Contradiction or row-budget exhaustion preserve evidence and force abstention; explicit reconstruction permits a wider budget/family.
- `bgr90/adequacy.py` separates *conditional n<=4 evidence* from public certainty. Without externally validated model-scope attestation, public certificates ALWAYS abstain; this does NOT solve automatic OOD detection. A demo-only Boolean flag is NOT secure attestation.
- `bgr90/quotient.py` performs exact minimal Moore machine equivalence for complete reference machines, not yet learned incomplete worlds; independent oracle enumerates future words to verify. `scripts/open_world_counterexample.py` constructs two larger machines consistent with any finite history but disagreeing next.
- Source-frozen registered study on 504 cases ×44 actions = 22,176 steps; 7 families with n=2,3,4,5 and nonstationarity. On 72 four-state cases, old n<=3 false conditional certificates=78 and new n<=4 false=0; on n=5+drifting worlds new conditional errors=140; public certificates=0 via total unattested abstention. The 4-state support remained consistent in 72/72 cases whereas old model contradicted 71/72.
- Independently reconstructed all 22,176 action observations and 6,103 conditional certificates. Source frozen under `experiments/SOURCE_FREEZE.json`; full study `results/study-001/`. Complete-machine quotient independently verified on 120 reference worlds, 17 had a smaller minimal predictive count.
